# Village Turn Example

## Diagrama del tablero de Village

La ilustración muestra el tablero de la aldea y señala estos elementos:

- **Tier 1 Shops**: tiendas de nivel 1.
- **Price tag**: etiqueta de precio.
- **Empty Chip space that will be refilled when any shop is completely empty**: espacio de ficha vacío que se repone cuando cualquier tienda queda completamente vacía.
- **The Tier 3 shop doesn't open until Round 2**: la tienda de nivel 3 no abre hasta la ronda 2.

## Village Turn Example

### 1

Nyx starts their 1st Village turn with 11 gold. They use their Browse action to peek at the Tier 2 Gem chip, and sees a Coin on the reverse. They return the chip to the space, Coin side up.

Nyx uses their shop action to buy that chip. Nyx starts with the skill Treasure Hound, giving them a discount of 1 gold on Coin chips, so they pay 7 for it, leaving them with 4 Gold. Then it is the next player's turn. Nyx does not refill the space they bought the chip from.

### 2

On their 2nd turn, Nyx peeks at the Artifact chip, hoping to find another Coin for a discount. It's a Shield, so they return it with the Artifact side up (so other players don't get that information).

For their shop action, Nyx visits the Healer's Hut. They recover 1 health for free, and pay 1 gold to recover an additional health, leaving 3 Gold.

### 3

With their final turn, they peek at the Tier 2 Potion chip. They can't afford it, but the information may be useful for next round's Village phase. With their discount, they can buy the Chest chip in the Tier 1 shop.

Since a shop is now completely empty, at the end of their turn they refill all the empty spaces on the Village board (except the Tier 3 spaces--that shop won't be open until next round).

# Game End

The game ends during the Path Phase when there are no more Path cards to reveal.

- Gain **3 VP** for each skill you unlocked.
- Add together your **Health, XP, and Gold**. For every **5** of the combined total, you gain **1 VP**.

*For example:* Nyx unlocked 3 skills, so gains 9 VP. Nyx also has 7 gold, 4 XP, and 2 health, for a total of 13. Nyx gains 2 VP, ignoring the remainder.

The player with the most Valor Points (VP) wins the game. In the event of a tie, the player with the most gold wins. If still tied, the tied players share the victory.
