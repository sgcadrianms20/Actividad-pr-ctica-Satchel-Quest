# 2 - Chests 🔴

**Check to see if you opened each chest and whether you disarmed the trap. Take any damage first, then collect rewards.**

To open a chest, you must match at least the number of required chips. If **opened**, chests grant their rewards, but inflict damage if not **disarmed**. To disarm a chest, you must match the required chips **exactly**, no more, no less.

## Chest Requirements

The icons along the top of the chip indicate the chips the chest requires to be opened. Each icon indicates that 1 chip of that type must be adjacent to the Chest — the strength of the chip does not matter.

> For example, a chest showing 2 weapon symbols cannot be solved by a single 2-strength sword. Two weapon chips need to be placed adjacent to it.

**Chest B 041A**
- Requirements: 2 Weapons & 1 Potion
- Reward (if opened): 4
- Damage (if not opened exactly): 1

## Rewards and Damage

- 🎁 Rewards you receive if the chest is **opened**.
- 🔴 The damage you take if the chest is not **disarmed**.

For each chest, there are 3 possible outcomes. If the surrounding 8 spaces:

- **Do not** contain the required chips, the chest is neither opened or disarmed. Take damage and gain no rewards.
- **Contain the required chips in excess** of the number shown, the chest is opened but not disarmed. Take damage, but collect the rewards.
- **Contain the required chips in exactly** the amount shown, the chest is opened, and the trap is disarmed. Take no damage and collect the rewards!

First take all damage from chests that were not disarmed. If your health drops to zero or below, refer to the **Dying** section (page 15). Then collect the rewards from chests you opened.

## Chest Examples

| Example | Adjacent chips | Opened? | Disarmed? | Explanation |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| 1 | Exactly 2 Weapons + 1 Potion | ✅ | ✅ | This chest is adjacent to exactly 2 weapons and 1 Potion, so it is **opened** and **disarmed**. |
| 2 | 2 Weapons + 2 Potions | ✅ | ❌ | This chest is adjacent to 2 weapons and 2 Potions. It meets the requirements and is **opened**. However, that is 1 more Potion than it requires, so it is **not disarmed**. |
| 3 | 1 Weapon + 1 Potion | ❌ | ❌ | This chest is adjacent to 1 Weapon and 1 Potion, which is 1 less Weapon than it requires. It is **not opened** and is **not disarmed**. |
