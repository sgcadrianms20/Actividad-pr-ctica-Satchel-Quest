# 9

## Gameplay: Resolution

Players resolve each step of the Resolution phase in order together.  
The steps are listed in order with reminders on the back of each player's skill reference.

> We recommend one player reads them aloud and waits for each player to finish before advancing to the next step.

### 0 - Path Cards

Calculate bonus VP (if any) for the current Path.

Some Path cards reward players for completing their dungeons in certain ways. These are evaluated at the beginning of the Resolution Phase.

## Phase 2: Resolution

### 1 - Potions

**Gain health equal to the total strength of your potions.**

Add up the strength of all the potions in your dungeon and gain that amount of health by advancing the token on the health track on your player board.

You may not exceed **10 health** — for each point you would gain above 10, instead gain an XP.

## Dungeon Play Example — Continued

Nyx continues to draw, placing chips on the 1st floor. When they draw their 4th chip, they have a decision to make: place it on the empty space A and finish the floor, or on the Potion optional feature B.

Nyx places it on the empty space, finishing the floor.

Now Nyx has another choice to make: continue to the next floor, or stop and leave the dungeon. They choose to continue, so they immediately advance their Hero figure and continue drawing.

### Continued example

Nyx finishes the third floor of their Dungeon Entrance map. They check to see if they have chips left in their bag. They do, so they immediately draw a Dungeon Depths map, choose a side, and place it below their current map.

They shift the Depths map one space to the right (so the Sword feature A is hitting the monster).

After placing the Depths map, they now decide whether to stop or continue. They again choose to continue. They move their figure to the first floor of the Depths, draw their next chip, and continue their adventure.

If they finish the Depths map, they'll get to draw another Depths!
