# PHASE 6: VILLAGE

Beginning with the starting player and proceeding clockwise, players take turns shopping. On each turn a player may buy a new chip from an open shop or visit one of the specialty shops. The phase ends when all players have taken 3 turns.

## CHIP SHOPS
There are 4 different **chip** shops in the village—two tier 1 shops, a tier 2 shop, and a tier 3 shop. The tier 3 shop begins the game closed, and opens in round 2. The shops offer the matching tier of chips. **Players may not look at the face-down side of the chips in shops.**

At the start of the Village phase, fill each empty space in the open shops by taking chips of the matching tier from the **bottom of that chip stack** and placing them in the shops without looking at the face-down side.

## A VILLAGE TURN
During your Village Turn you may perform one **browse** action, and then one **shop** action.

## BROWSE ACTIONS
You may choose one of the two browse actions:

1. **Peek at a Chip:** Look at the face-down side of a single chip in secret, then return it with the side of your choice face-up.
   *Note: You are not required to purchase this chip after peeking.*

2. **Refresh a Shop:** Discard any **tool** to remove all the chips from one shop and refill them. *Place the removed chips on top of an existing stack of that tier.*

# SHOP ACTIONS

## VISITING A SPECIALTY SHOP
You may visit one of the other three shops instead of purchasing a chip. To do so, pay any required costs, and collect the reward. You may only gain the reward once per visit, but you may visit the same shop more than once in the phase.

<!-- Espacio para la imagen indicativa de las tiendas -->
*Tier 2 Shop*

**Gurdy's Gear:** Gain any 2 tools of your choice for 1 gold, or 3 tools of your choice for 2 gold.

**Healer's Hut:** Recover 1 health for free, and you may pay 1 gold to recover 1 additional health. The most you can heal from one visit is 2 health.

**Pterry's Tavern:** Gain 2 gold and 1 Torch tool.

## BUYING A CHIP
Choose one chip, pay the amount of gold shown on the price tag to the supply, and place the chip into your bag. You may look at both sides of the chip after purchasing it.

*Note: Each Hero receives a discount on certain types of chips, but only if the face-up side matches the discounted type.*

If the purchased chip was the last chip in the shop, **refill all empty spaces in every open shop**, not just the shop you purchased from (don't discard any chips). Otherwise, do not refill empty shop spaces after making a purchase.

## END OF THE VILLAGE PHASE
The Village Phase ends when each player has taken 3 turns.
